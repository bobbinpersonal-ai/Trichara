Not all vinyl is equal. Here's what we wrap with — and why.

A wrap is only as good as the film it's printed on. We work exclusively with premium cast vinyl from the manufacturers the industry trusts — the same films used by the best shops in the world. Here's our lineup, and our honest take on each.

3M

Premium

Flagship: 2080 Series · Color Flip · 2080 protective cap layer on gloss

  • 3.5 mil dual-cast film
  • Controltac™ + Comply™ air-release — repositionable, bubble-free
  • Up to 8 years vertical durability
  • 60-inch rolls, 100+ colors and finishes

Our take: The industry benchmark. When longevity and a flawless finish matter most, 3M 2080 is the safest bet in wrapping — and the 2080 Flip series gives you factory-grade color-shift. This is our premium tier.

3m.com →

Avery Dennison

Premium

Flagship: Supreme Wrapping Film (SW900) · ColorFlow™ color-shift series

  • 3.2 mil dual-layer cast film
  • Easy Apply™ RS — repositionable, slideable, long-term removable
  • 100+ colors; satin and ColorFlow finishes are best-in-class
  • Removes cleanly for years — great for leased vehicles

Our take: The installer's favorite. Avery's adhesive is the most forgiving in the business, which means sharper edges and cleaner recesses on your car. ColorFlow is our go-to recommendation for color-shift wraps.

graphics.averydennison.com →

TeckWrap

Value

Flagship: Chameleon / color-shift films · hundreds of colors and finishes

  • Huge catalog — some of the wildest color-shifts on the market
  • Air-release adhesive for clean installs
  • Accessible pricing without looking cheap

Our take: The color playground. If you want a head-turning chameleon wrap without the premium price tag, TeckWrap is our mid-tier pick — maximum drama per dollar.

teckwrap.com →

KPMF

Pro

Flagship: K75400 Series cast wrapping film

  • British-made cast film with air-release technology
  • Excellent conformability around curves and recesses
  • Strong color range across gloss, matte, satin and metallic

Our take: Europe's quiet giant. KPMF doesn't shout as loud as the big two, but installers love how it handles — a superb all-rounder we reach for often.

kpmf.com →

ORACAL by Orafol

Premium

Flagship: 970RA RapidAir® wrapping cast

  • Premium cast film with RapidAir® air-egress
  • Among the longest-rated durabilities in the industry
  • Deep, rich color — a favorite for fleet and commercial work

Our take: The durability king. When a wrap needs to survive years of sun, highway miles, or fleet duty, 970RA is what we spec.

oracal.com →

Hexis

Pro

Flagship: HX30000 Series cast film

  • French-manufactured cast vinyl, a staple of European wrap shops
  • Hexis TakeOff™ adhesive options for clean removability
  • Strong in textured and special-effect finishes

Our take: Precision French engineering. Hexis is the connoisseur's choice — especially strong when you want texture and special effects done right.

hexis.com →

Inozetek

Premium

  • Thick, premium construction for a true paint-like gloss
  • Rich metallics with exceptional depth
  • A favorite for show cars and exotics
