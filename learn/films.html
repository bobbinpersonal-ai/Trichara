Vinyl wrap finishes,
explained.

Gloss, satin, matte, chrome, color-shift — what each finish actually looks like and what it costs.

Every wrap starts with the film. Here's the honest breakdown of each finish type — look, price tier, and what to know before you pick.

Try finishes on a car →
$$ · Most popular

Gloss

Looks like a fresh paint job — deep, wet shine. The safest pick if you want a new color without explaining yourself.

Best for: daily drivers, resale-friendly color changes.
Know: shows swirl marks like paint does; keep it washed.

$$ · Modern

Satin

Halfway between gloss and matte — a soft sheen that makes body lines pop. The "expensive" look right now.

Best for: sports cars, Teslas, anything with curves.
Know: hides minor imperfections better than gloss.

$$ · Stealth

Matte

Flat, zero shine. Murdered-out black, military greens, matte grays — the stealth look.

Best for: trucks, Wranglers, anyone going for aggressive.
Know: can't be polished — scratches stay. Wash gently.

$$$$ · Showstopper

Chrome

Mirror finish. Gold, silver, rose gold — the loudest wrap you can buy. People will film your car at red lights.

Best for: show cars, exotics, marketing vehicles.
Know: priciest film, shows every flaw underneath, needs careful washing.

$$$$ · Head-turner

Color-shift

Chameleon films that flip between two or three colors as light hits them — purple-to-teal, green-to-gold. Avery ColorFlow and 3M Flip lead this category.

Best for: standing out at meets and on the street.
Know: premium price, and panel alignment matters — this is expert-installer territory.

$$$ · Textured

Carbon fiber

Real woven texture you can feel. Usually used on hoods, roofs, mirrors, and spoilers rather than full cars.

Best for: accents and aero parts.
Know: texture makes full-car installs labor-intensive.

$$$ · Textured

Brushed metal

Brushed aluminum look — steel, black, bronze. Industrial and premium at the same time.

Best for: Teslas, European sedans, full wraps with depth.
Know: the grain direction has to run the same way on every panel.

$$$ · Custom

Printed graphics

Anything you can design, we can print — camo, anime liveries, business branding, full fleet graphics.

Best for: businesses, semis, one-of-one builds.
Know: printed film gets a protective laminate; design time is part of the quote.

How long does wrap last?

With decent care, 5–7 years for quality films from 3M, Avery Dennison, and similar. Sun is the enemy — garaged cars last longest. See our wrap care guide.

Yes — call (424) 426-0760 and we'll bring swatches. Photos don't do color-shift justice.
