Why protect your paint?

Rock chips are expensive

Repainting a chipped bumper runs $500–$1,500. A hood respray can top $2,000. Film costs less than one repaint — and prevents all of them.

Resale value

Original factory paint is worth real money at trade-in. A car with clean, protected paint appraises higher than one with a resprayed front end.

It heals itself

Light scratches and swirls disappear with heat from the sun or engine. The film we install is self-healing — that's not marketing, it's the top coat chemistry.

Honest take: PPF isn't cheap. It's cheaper than repainting. If you drive highways, own a dark-colored car, or plan to keep it 5+ years, the math works.

Common questions

How long does installation take?

A partial or full front package takes about half a day. Track packages take a full day. Full body wraps take 3 to 5 days. We do it all at your driveway or office — you don't go anywhere.

How long does the film last?

The XPEL film we install carries a 10-year warranty against yellowing, peeling, and cracking. In real-world daily driving, expect 7 to 10 years of protection.

Can I wash my car normally?

Yes — wash it like normal paint. Just avoid abrasive compounds, and keep pressure washers away from film edges for the first week while everything fully sets.

What if the film peels or bubbles?

Call us. Installation issues are on us — we'll come back and fix any lifting edge or bubble at no charge. That's part of the warranty, not a favor.

Does it work on any car or color?

Yes. Any make, any color — the film is optically clear and invisible on gloss paint. Dark colors benefit most, since chips show worst on black, blue, and red.

Do I need to bring my car anywhere?

No. That's the whole point. We bring film, tools, and a canopy to your home or office. You hand us the keys and go about your day.
